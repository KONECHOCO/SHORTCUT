# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Shortcut Atlas is an educational reference app for Windows and Mac keyboard shortcuts. The app does not require an account, does not let users create content, and does not collect contact information directly.

## Shortcut Atlas does not track you

The app does not link data collected from it with third-party data for advertising purposes, and does not share data with data brokers. It does not request or use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID) to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Data Used by Advertising Partners

Shortcut Atlas uses Unity Ads, via Unity LevelPlay mediation, to show non-personalized ads. Advertising partners may process technical information — device type, language, country, approximate location derived from IP address, ad-impression and click events, and diagnostics — to deliver and measure ads within this app. This data is used only for serving and measuring ads in Shortcut Atlas, not to build a cross-app or cross-company profile of you.

See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) for details.

## Data We Do Not Collect Directly

The app does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, precise GPS location, payment information, health information, or user-generated content. Favorites and preferences are stored only on your device (local storage).

## Links

The app may include links to support, legal, or privacy resources. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## User Choices

The app shows a privacy options screen for advertising consent (e.g. GDPR), reachable from the app's header (lock icon).

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support resources provided on the app's store listing.
